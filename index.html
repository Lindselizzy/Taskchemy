What do I do with this?

Project management for your personal life.

The Squeeze uses project-management thinking such as dependencies, parallel work, resource allocation, constraints, buffers and replanning to turn everyday chaos into a workable path. You get the logic without the paperwork.

Use The Squeeze when the clock starts feeling rude.

You have a pile of things to do and a time they need to be handled by. Dump the lemons. The Squeeze tells you what to do first, overlaps anything that can run in the background, and rebuilds when life gets in the way.

Keep it simple first.

Most plans only need task names, rough minutes, and a finish time. People, fixed events, travel, priorities, and dependencies are optional.
