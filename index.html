What do I do with this?

When should I use The Squeeze?

Use it when you have several things to do and a real time you need to be finished. Add the tasks, give rough estimates, and follow one next move at a time.

Use The Squeeze when the clock starts feeling rude.

You have a pile of things to do and a real time you need to be done by. Dump the lemons. The Squeeze tells you what to do first, overlaps anything that can run in the background, and rebuilds when life gets in the way.

Keep it simple first.

Most plans only need task names, rough minutes, and a finish time. People, fixed events, travel, priorities, and dependencies are optional.
